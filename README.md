# Hospital-Administration-Analysis
1. Project Title / Headline -
You are a data analyst in a large hospital's administration department in Novartis, focusing on improving patient care while reducing unnecessary readmissions. Recently, the hospital has seen a spike in readmission rates, leading to increased healthcare costs and indicating potential quality of care issues. The administration is concerned about the impact on patient outcomes and the hospital's reputation. Your task is to analyze hospital admission data to identify patterns and factors associated with high readmission rates.

2. Short Description / Purpose -
Addressing high readmission rates is crucial for enhancing patient care quality, optimizing hospital resource allocation, and complying with healthcare regulations. Identifying the underlying causes can lead to targeted interventions, improved patient outcomes, and potentially significant cost savings.


3. Tech Stack - Excel

4.  Features / Highlights -Built a hospital readmissions dashboard to identify which patient groups, specialties, and treatment factors drive readmissions, helping care teams target interventions and reduce repeat admissions. Tracks readmission rate and length of stay across age groups, medical specialties, and diabetic medication changes.

Walkthrough of Key Visuals - 
* KPI Cards — Overall Readmission Rate (46.2%) and Average Length of Stay (4.4 days) give a top-level view of hospital performance
* Readmission Rate by Age Group (Bar Chart) — Compares readmission rates across 10 age bands (5-95), showing readmissions climb steadily with age — lowest at 19% (age 5) and peaking around 47-48% (ages 65-85)
* Top 10 Average Length of Stay by Specialty (Bar Chart) — Ranks medical specialties by average hospital stay, with Pediatrics shortest (~1 day) and Physical Medicine & Rehab longest (10.1 days)
* Readmission Rate vs Diabetic Medication Change (Donut Chart) — Splits patients by whether their diabetic medication changed, showing readmission split (0.4 No / 0.5 Yes) — flags medication changes as a possible readmission factor
* Diagnoses vs Length of Stay vs Readmission (Table) — Cross-tabs number of diagnoses against average time in hospital and readmission %, showing readmission rate generally rises with diagnosis count (from 23% at 1 diagnosis to 60-67% at 11-14 diagnoses)
* Interactive Filters — Age midpoint and medical specialty slicers let users drill into readmission patterns for specific patient segments
